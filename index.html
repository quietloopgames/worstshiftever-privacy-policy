Worst Shift Ever
Privacy Policy

Android closed test · Last updated October 6, 2026

Worst Shift Ever is a game by Quiet Loop Games. This policy covers the Android closed-test version. The iPhone version has a separate privacy policy.

Game progress and backup

Your Tips, unlocked characters and cosmetics, upgrades, achievements, career totals, recent shifts, and settings are stored on your device. This Android test has no in-game account, developer-operated server, or in-game cloud save. Android's device backup or device-transfer features may back up or restore app data depending on your device and Google account settings. Quiet Loop Games does not operate that backup service. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes the local copy, although a device backup may persist under your backup settings.

Google services in this test

This closed-test version does not offer Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, the Supporter purchase, or advertising. Its one revive per shift is free. These features may be added in a later version; this policy will be updated before they are enabled. Google Play may separately process installation, update, and other store-related information under Google's own privacy policy.

Advertising SDK included in the app

Although the closed test does not request or display ads, the Android app package includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and a device advertising-ID permission. According to Google, this SDK can automatically collect and share IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the advertising ID where available, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Such data is handled by Google and its partners; Quiet Loop Games does not receive your full payment information or operate a separate analytics server. Google's SDK states that it encrypts the data it transmits. The app does not offer an advertising consent or privacy-options screen in this test because it does not request ads.

Permissions and third parties

The app has internet access and includes an advertising-ID permission. Core gameplay does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Google handles information according to its Privacy Policy; see also Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Your choices, retention, and deletion

You can play this test without signing in or making a purchase. You can remove locally stored game progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. A copy made by Android's backup service may remain until you manage or delete it through your device and Google account settings. Google manages information it processes under its own policy and settings. For privacy questions or requests about information controlled by Quiet Loop Games, contact us below.

Children and updates

The game is intended for a general audience and has no chat or player-to-player messaging. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us. We will revise this policy and its date when the Android game's services or data handling change.
